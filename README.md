# Industry Talk 1
## From Developer to CEO: The Perfect Blend of Technology and Business

### 1. Activity Information
- **Course:** Technology and Information System (SECP1513)
- **Event:** Industry Talk 1
- **Date:** 26 October 2026
- **Speaker:** Assoc. Prof. Dr. Hj. Mohamad Hafis Izran bin Ishak
- **Position:** CEO of AR Mechatronics PLT and myReds
- **Group:** Group 4 – Milo

### 2. Introduction
This industry talk focused on the importance of combining technology and business skills in the ICT industry.

The speaker shared his career journey and explained how technical knowledge, business understanding and leadership skills contribute to building successful technology products and businesses.

### 3. Executive Summary
The talk highlighted the importance of combining technical knowledge with business skills.

Technology alone is not enough to build a successful product. Developers and business leaders must also understand user needs, business operations, costs and long-term sustainability.

The main message is that technology should solve real-world problems and create value for users through continuous improvement.

### 4. Content of the Talk

**4.1 The Developer Paradox**

- Developers mainly focus on completing technical work.
- CEOs focus on long-term operations and business growth.
- Both technical and management skills are important.

**4.2 TIS in Practice**

- Technology should solve real-world problems.
- Users need convenience, while buyers consider return on investment (ROI).
- Security and transparency are also important.

**4.3 AI and Human**

- AI can help speed up processes and improve efficiency.
- Human trust, experience and accountability remain important.

**4.4 Question and Answer Session**

One of the questions discussed was the biggest challenge when moving from a developer role to a CEO role.

The discussion highlighted the importance of obtaining funding, including convincing clients to pay for services or renew subscriptions.

### 6. Reflection

Through this industry talk, I learned that working in the ICT industry involves more than just writing code.

I realized that understanding business costs, maintenance, risks and user needs is important when developing a successful product.

The example of the myReds application showed how technology can connect residents, visitors, security guards and management through a single platform.

I also learned that responsibility does not stop after launching an application. Data privacy and system reliability must continue to be maintained.

Although AI can improve efficiency, human accountability and experience are still essential.

Overall, this talk motivated me to improve both my technical knowledge and business understanding as a Computer Science student.
